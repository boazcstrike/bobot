# 06 - 90 Day Roadmap

## Month 1 - Foundation + Validation
- Finalize ICP and positioning statement
- Build profile and content identity
- Create v1 lead magnet
- Build v1 low-ticket product
- Publish first 20-24 content pieces
- Start DM-first sales process

Binary checks:
- Product live: yes/no
- Funnel live: yes/no
- First 5 sales: yes/no

## Month 2 - Proof + Conversion
- Improve hooks and content retention
- Collect testimonials and mini case studies
- Refine product packaging and sales page
- Increase story selling consistency

Binary checks:
- 10+ total sales: yes/no
- 3+ testimonials: yes/no
- Positive DM conversion trend: yes/no

## Month 3 - Stabilize System
- Lock top-performing content formats
- Introduce light upsell or bundle
- Standardize weekly production and sales rhythm
- Optional small paid growth experiments

Binary checks:
- Weekly publishing cadence stable: yes/no
- Revenue trend upward for 4 weeks: yes/no
- Process documented and repeatable: yes/no
