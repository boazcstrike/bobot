# 07 - Weeks 13 to 24 Scale Plan

## Goal
Transition from validation to consistent revenue engine and hit $2,000/month by month 6.

## Strategic Moves
1. Double down on top 2 content pillars only.
2. Increase sales frequency while preserving trust.
3. Expand offer value through version upgrades, not new product sprawl.
4. Add collaborations with adjacent creators serving corporate audiences.

## Revenue Math Reference
If product price is $39:
- $2,000/month needs ~52 sales/month
- ~13 sales/week

## Scale Levers
- Better conversion from existing traffic
- Higher profile visits from stronger hooks
- DM-to-sale script optimization
- Small upsell adoption

## Execution Checkpoints
- Month 4: 30-40 sales/month pace
- Month 5: 40-50 sales/month pace
- Month 6: 52+ sales/month pace

## Risk Controls
- If growth stalls for 3 consecutive weeks: revisit hooks and ICP language.
- If conversion stalls: tighten offer promise and social proof.
