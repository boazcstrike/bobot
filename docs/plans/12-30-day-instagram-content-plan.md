## Objective

## Monthly Repeat System (Simple)
Use this 7-day loop and repeat it 4 times, then run Day 29-30 as optimization days.

- Day 1: Reel (pain + hook)
- Day 2: Carousel (framework)
- Day 3: Reel (myth-busting)

## One Story Strategy (Use Daily): 5-Frame ARC
Use one 5-frame story sequence every day.
1. **A - Attention**: relatable pain or scenario
2. **R - Reframe**: short perspective shift
3. **C - Conversion**: quick action + CTA (DM keyword or link)
4. **Proof frame**: screenshot/win/testimonial/process snippet
5. **CTA frame**: "Reply EXECUTE" or "Get the $39 Execution OS"
