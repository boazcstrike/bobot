# 00 - Overview and Principles

## Strategic Objective
Build a creator-led Instagram business that sells digital products to working professionals and reaches $2,000/month in 6 months.

## Core Reality
You currently have:
- No recent active IG business footprint
- No validated paid outcomes yet
- Strong execution advantage as a developer
- Sufficient time (20h/week) and budget ($1,000/month)

This means phase 1 is not scaling. Phase 1 is **clarity + proof + first sales**.

## Working Assumptions
- Your best first market is professionals who feel overwhelmed and want practical systems.
- Your strongest differentiation is combining mindset + developer-level system thinking.
- Voice-led, face-optional short video is sustainable for you.

## Operating Principles
1. Simplicity over complexity: one audience, one flagship low-ticket offer, one core funnel.
2. Evidence over opinion: let weekly KPIs decide pivots.
3. Build assets once, reuse repeatedly (content -> lead magnet -> product snippets).
4. Conversion discipline: every content theme must map to a product promise.

## Constraints and Mitigation
- Time: use content batching + strict weekly calendar.
- Audience growth: prioritize short-form hooks and collaborative reach.
- Offer creation: launch v1 fast, iterate with buyer feedback.
- Sales: set weekly conversion experiments (CTA, DM scripts, landing copy).

## Immediate Direction
Start with a problem-solution lane:
- Problem: working professionals struggle with consistency, focus, and execution.
- Solution: structured systems (templates, routines, automations) blending mindset + practical execution.
