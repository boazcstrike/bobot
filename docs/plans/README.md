# Instagram Digital Product Plan - Master Index

## Purpose
Build a practical, execution-focused strategy to reach **$2,000/month within 6 months** from Instagram using digital products.

## Direction Update
The repository now also includes a product-direction pivot for Bobot as a personal PC assistant. For engineering execution, treat this as the active product plan:
- `14-bobot-personal-assistant-platform.md`

## Founder Context Summary
- Time capacity: 20 hours/week
- Skills to package: coding, mindset
- Existing proof/results: none yet (must create proof through pilot outcomes)
- Audience idea: working individuals/corporate professionals
- Revenue target: $2,000/month in 6 months
- Sales history: none yet
- Preferred first offer: low-ticket template/guide
- Tool comfort: high (developer-level implementation)
- Budget: PHP 50,000/month (~$1,000/month)
- Content style: prefers voice-only + video; best channels are Reels, carousels, stories
- Core constraints: time, audience growth, offer creation, sales
- Brand intent: lifestyle + freedom outcome

## Planning Architecture
This plan is intentionally layered:
1. **High-level strategy and positioning**
2. **Offer and funnel architecture**
3. **Content operating system**
4. **Execution calendar and KPIs**
5. **Detailed weekly playbook**

## Success Definition
By end of month 6:
- Stable content production at planned cadence
- Clear low-ticket offer-market fit signal
- Repeatable weekly sales motion
- Average monthly digital product revenue at or above **$2,000**
