# 01 - Audience and Niche

## Is "working individuals/corporate" too vague?
Yes, for selling it is too broad. It is acceptable as a starting pool, but your offer and content need a tighter ICP.

## Recommended Primary ICP (First 90 Days)
**Ambitious corporate professionals (ages 24-38), English-speaking, mid-level roles, digitally literate, overwhelmed by workload and inconsistent personal execution.**

## Pain Points
- Mental overload and poor weekly planning
- Inconsistent routines and follow-through
- Career growth feels slow despite effort
- Lack of practical systems for execution

## Desired Outcomes
- Better focus and consistency
- Clear weekly structure
- Personal productivity that survives busy schedules
- Tangible progress in career and personal goals

## Positioning Statement (Draft)
I help busy professionals install simple mindset + execution systems so they can deliver more with less stress.

## Niche Test Matrix
Evaluate content pillars by:
- Reach potential
- Buyer pain intensity
- Your credibility to teach
- Ease of productization

Keep only pillars scoring high in all 4.

## Initial Pillars
1. Focus systems for busy professionals
2. Mindset frameworks for execution under pressure
3. Practical productivity templates and workflows
