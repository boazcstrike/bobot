# 08 - KPI Scorecard

## North Star
Monthly digital product revenue

## Weekly KPI Set
- Content output count (reels/carousels/stories)
- Reel average watch time
- Profile visits
- Link clicks
- DM inquiries
- Sales count
- Revenue
- Refund rate

## Weekly Target Ranges (Early Stage)
- Reels: 4/week
- Carousels: 2/week
- Stories: daily
- DM inquiries: 15+/week
- Sales: 3-8/week (ramp target)

## Review Cadence
- Weekly review: Friday
- Monthly strategic review: last business day of month

## Decision Rules
- High reach + low sales: improve offer/CTA clarity
- Low reach + stable conversion: improve hooks/distribution
- Low reach + low conversion: revisit ICP and positioning

## Dashboard Format
Use one sheet with weekly rows and monthly summary blocks.
