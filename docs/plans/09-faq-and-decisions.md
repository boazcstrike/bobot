# 09 - FAQ and Decisions

## Q: Personal brand vs theme page?
- Personal brand: your identity, voice, and perspective are central. Higher trust and conversion, usually better for coaching/product authority.
- Theme page: content-first page with less personal identity. Easier to scale output but often weaker trust and lower conversion for expert offers.

Recommendation for your case:
Start as a **personal brand with voice-first content**. It matches your comfort and improves conversion for mindset + execution products.

## Q: Is the target audience still too broad?
Yes for now. Start with one primary segment:
- Mid-level corporate professionals dealing with overwhelm and inconsistency.

## Q: What if no one buys early?
Run 2-week diagnosis:
1. Check offer-message fit
2. Review CTA clarity
3. Audit DM script quality
4. Add stronger proof (pilot outcomes)

## Q: Should we run paid ads immediately?
No. First validate organic conversion flow, then amplify what already works.

## Q: Can coding be part of the offer now?
Only lightly. Keep the first offer mostly productivity/mindset-system focused. Add coding-heavy assets later if audience demand appears.
